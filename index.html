01

Executive summary

Smartland has strong raw material for paid search: a distinctive boutique, nature-based offer (Bush & Beach Kinder), chef-made meals, an all-inclusive fee structure and consistently warm Google reviews. The problem is not the offer. It is that the tracking cannot tell the ten centres apart and puts no value on any lead. Fixing this before the campaigns go live will make every dollar of the short seasonal budget measurable.

Key findings

01

One form conversion for ten centres. “Book A Tour - Form” is the only lead-form action (420 conversions, $0.00 value) feeding 43 of 43 campaigns. We cannot see which centre produced a lead, or bid differently by centre.

02

No consistent values. Form leads carry $0. Calls from ads (598) and Google-hosted clicks to call (467) carry a flat $1 each. Bidding sees calls as “worth” more than forms by accident, not by design.

03

Only two website phone numbers are tracked (07 5479 4111 and 07 5444 4800) and both show “Awaiting conversions”. The other centres’ numbers have no website call action.

04

Irrelevant and overlapping “Primary” actions. Menu views (a restaurant-style local action), email clicks and Google-hosted clicks to call are all Primary and flagged “Needs attention”. Three of the four goals are not even attached to the campaigns.

05

Auction insights show room to grow. Smartland has 15.95% impression share. Guardian and Goodstart each sit under 10% and rank above Smartland when both appear (59.80% and 45.30% of the time), so ad copy and rank need to earn the click.

3.2 Recommended structure

Build three primary actions per centre so each of the ten campaigns reports, and is valued, on its own:

Action (per centre)How it firesNotes
SL | Tour Request | [Centre]Form submission, with the centre read from the “Which Centre” fieldCount: One. Thank-you event or dataLayer push (generate_lead) with a centre parameter. Never pass the child’s name or age to any ad tag.
SL | Call from Ads | [Centre]Call asset on that campaignSet the call asset’s conversion action to this centre’s action; minimum call length 60 seconds.
SL | Call from Website | [Centre]Dedicated PPC phone number on that centre’s landing pageGoogle forwarding number or call-tracking number; capture GCLID.
Make only these primary: Tour Request and qualified calls (60 seconds or more). Move Clicks to call, Email and any 3D-tour or engagement actions to Secondary (reporting only). Remove Menu views. This keeps bidding focused on real tour intent.

3.3 Giving each action a value

Values do not need to be perfect, but they must be deliberate and consistent. Suggested method, to agree with the client:

  • Lead value = enrolment probability × annual revenue per enrolled child. Inputs needed per centre: tour-to-enrolment rate, average days per week booked, average daily fee, and typical length of stay.
  • Give calls of 60 seconds or more a higher value than forms, since they usually book a tour faster, then give each centre its own value (a full centre needs fewer leads than an under-filled one).
  • Once values exist, switch the campaigns from Maximise clicks and Maximise conversions to Maximise conversion value so budget leans toward higher-value centres.
  • Optional phase 2: import “Enrolled” as an offline conversion via GCLID from the enrolment system. This is the best-practice signal but is not required for the first season.

3.4 Dedicated phone numbers for PPC landing pages

We are building a separate landing page per centre (and per service where it applies), the same approach used for Auto King and Pink Slip Today. The numbers on the main website are needed for SEO and Google Business Profile consistency, so we need separate numbers for the PPC pages. Two ways to do it:

OptionHow it worksTrade-off
A. Google forwarding numbersGoogle swaps in a tracking number on the landing page and forwards to the centre line. Calls over the set length count as conversions.Free, quick to set up; no call recording.
B. 10 dedicated numbers via a call-tracking providerOne forwarding number per centre, used only on PPC pages and call assets.Small monthly cost; adds call recording and “did they book a tour” outcome tagging.
Requirement: 10 numbers forwarding to each centre’s existing line (Option B), or sign-off on Option A. Either way, keep the PPC pages out of search results (noindex) so the original numbers stay the only ones Google sees for SEO.

3.5 Implementation checklist

  1. Add a dataLayer event with the centre name on successful submission of each landing page form.
  2. Create the 30 centre-specific actions (10 forms, 10 ads calls, 10 website calls), values, 30-day window, data-driven attribution.
  3. Re-classify Primary/Secondary; remove Menu views; archive unused legacy campaigns (43 campaigns are attached to the account-default goals).
  4. QA: submit a test enquiry for every centre and place a test call to every number; confirm Tag Assistant and Google Ads status “Recording conversions” before launch.

Tap-to-call trigger tagged with the centre; Google forwarding / dedicated PPC number tags on the landing pages; per-centre website call conversions.

Patterns that win clicks in this category

  • Book a tour is the universal call to action; none of the large providers ask for a full enrolment up front.
  • Time-bound enrolment language (“now enrolling”, “Tour Month”, “taking enrolments for the new year”) is common, so urgency alone will not differentiate; pair it with something only Smartland can say.
  • Cost reassurance (discounts on multiple days, fee assistance, subsidy) is used by the national providers. Smartland’s equivalent is “all-inclusive: nappies, wipes, meals, sunscreen”.
  • Smartland should own: boutique and family-owned, Bush & Beach Kinder, chef-made meals, 3D tours, daily photo updates, centre manager by name.
  • Use each centre’s current NQS rating: if a centre is “Exceeding”, that becomes a headline.

Rules for urgency and offers

  • Only use “places filling”, “limited places” or “secure your place” when the specific centre confirms availability. Use “Tours available” or “Enrol for 2027” where it is not.
  • Confirm whether any real offer exists (for example waived enrolment fee, sibling discount, refer-a-friend). Do not advertise one that has not been approved.
  • Use “Govt-approved kindergarten” only for Queensland centres; NSW wording differs, so write those separately.
  • Pin the brand headline in position 1 or 2 and the suburb headline in the other; leave the rest unpinned so Google can learn.
  • Assets to add: call asset (the centre’s tracked number), location asset (each centre’s Business Profile), sitelinks (Book a Tour, Kindergarten, What’s Included, 3D Tour, Reviews), callouts (Family-owned, Chef-made meals, Nappies & wipes included, Bush & Beach Kinder), structured snippets (programs), images (centre and outdoor spaces).
